We read everything and reply to as much as we can.

### General enquiries

Questions, corrections and kind words: [hello@neighbourhood.example](mailto:hello@neighbourhood.example)

### Pitches and contributors

We commission writers, photographers and illustrators based in the cities we cover. Send a short pitch (three or four sentences), a few links to your work and the city you know best to [hello@neighbourhood.example](mailto:hello@neighbourhood.example) with "Pitch" in the subject line.

### Partnerships and advertising

For brand partnerships, sponsored content and advertising, see [Partner With Us](/partner-with-us) or write to [partners@neighbourhood.example](mailto:partners@neighbourhood.example).

### Press

For press enquiries and interviews: [hello@neighbourhood.example](mailto:hello@neighbourhood.example)
