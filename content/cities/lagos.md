Lagos doesn't ask for your attention; it takes it. Between the gallery openings on Victoria Island, the tech studios of Yaba and the late-night suya spots of Surulere, this is a city built on hustle and hospitality in equal measure. Our guides are written by people who live here, so you can skip the traffic and get straight to the good parts.
