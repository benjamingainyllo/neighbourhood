Neighbourhood is an independent magazine for a new kind of traveller: the designer spending a month in Lagos, the photographer passing through Nairobi, the remote worker who chose Kigali because it felt right.

We publish city guides written by people who live there, conversations with the artists, chefs and founders shaping their cities, photo essays, personal essays by locals, and the hotels worth building a trip around.

## What we believe

African cities are some of the most exciting places in the world to visit, make things and meet people, and they deserve travel writing that's as thoughtful as the places themselves. We'd rather send you to one great neighbourhood than ten forgettable landmarks.

## How we work

Our writers and photographers are based in, or deeply connected to, the cities they cover. We pay them fairly. We never accept payment for positive coverage. When a story is produced with a partner, it's clearly labelled **Partnership**. Some articles include affiliate links, which are always disclosed.

## Where we are

We launched with Lagos, Nairobi and Kigali, with more cities coming soon. If you'd like to write for us, [get in touch](/contact).
