Kigali is often described as Africa's tidiest capital, which undersells it. Spread across a ridge of green hills, it's a city made for walking slowly, drinking excellent coffee and finding the small galleries, design shops and restaurants that are turning it into one of the continent's most interesting places to spend a week.
