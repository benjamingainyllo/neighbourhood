Every April the long rains arrive in Nairobi, and for a few weeks the city changes character. Streets empty in the afternoon downpour, then fill again at dusk under a washed, golden light. I spent the season walking from Westlands to the old town with one camera and one lens, photographing the city as it dried off.

These images are not about landmarks. They're about the in-between moments: the light on a wet road, a market packing up, a skyline that suddenly looks very close.

The best time to photograph Nairobi is the half hour after a storm. The dust is gone, the colours are saturated and everyone who has been sheltering comes back onto the street at once.
