Nairobi is where East Africa does business, but it's also one of the continent's most rewarding cities to simply spend time in. Start your morning with Kenyan coffee in Kilimani, spend the afternoon among the studios and galleries of Westlands, and watch giraffes against a skyline on the edge of town.
