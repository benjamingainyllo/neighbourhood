This policy explains what personal information Neighbourhood ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, why we collect it and what your rights are.

## Information we collect

- **Newsletter sign-ups:** if you subscribe, we collect your email address and pass it to our email provider to send you the newsletter.
- **Messages:** if you email us, we keep your message and contact details to reply.
- **Usage data:** our hosting provider and any analytics tools may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

## Advertising and cookies

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

## Affiliate links

Some articles contain affiliate links. If you click one, the partner may set cookies to track that a booking came from us. We do not receive your personal details from these partners.

## How long we keep information

We keep newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe, and messages for as long as needed to respond.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK and EU GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email [hello@neighbourhood.example](mailto:hello@neighbourhood.example).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
