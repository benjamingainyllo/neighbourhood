Neighbourhood readers are designers, founders, photographers, remote workers and curious travellers from the US, UK and Europe who are actively planning trips to African cities. They read slowly, they book thoughtfully, and they trust our recommendations.

We work with a small number of partners whose work our readers will genuinely value: hotels, airlines, tour operators, tourism boards and brands made on the continent.

## Ways to work together

### Partnership stories

Long-form features produced by our editorial team with your brand, in our voice and to our standards. Every partnership story is clearly labelled **Partnership**.

### City guide sponsorship

Present a city guide or an entire city section for a season.

### Newsletter

A single, clearly marked partner slot in The Neighbourhood Letter.

### Display advertising

Tasteful, limited ad placements within articles.

## Our standards

We only partner with businesses we'd recommend anyway. Partners never have approval over editorial content, and we always disclose paid relationships.
